Where does Nepal stand right now?

Uneven. Everyone uses chatbots. We sell only expertise and man hours. We own very few AI products. The paperwork got loud in the last twelve months. That is not an AI industry yet.

Adoption you can see

  • This room. Almost everyone here has used a chat model this week. ICT Meetup v7 was already AI-themed, January 2024.
  • Bank and tech company chatbots are getting common: Saathi at NIC Asia, eVA at eSewa., and more.
  • One campus survey of health students: 300 of 356 use generative AI often or always. one campus, 2026

What we actually sell

  • Data labelling: CloudFactory. That is how Nepal entered the supply chain.
  • Export SWE/AI: Leapfrog, Cotiviti, Verisk, Wiseyak, Deerwalk / Cedar Gate.
  • Talent + education: Fusemachines. Fellowship, then Nasdaq FUSE.
  • Cyber / experts in the loop: SecurityPal. Nepal bench, foreign buyers.
  • Research: NAAMII, plus Nepali-language papers. A lab, not a product industry.
  • Thin local product: Second Eye / ChestGuru at TUTH. A trial, not a product industry.

What the state did

  • National AI Policy, Aug 2025. Targets: 5,000 AI professionals, top-50 on a global scorecard, AI literacy in schools.
  • National AI Centre, 10 Nov 2025. Couldn't find what exactly does this Center do.
  • Central bank AI rules for banks, Dec 2025. Short rulebook focusing on risks, governance and compliance.
  • Budget line for a state AI computer centre, 29 May 2026. Announced, not built.

What Numbers I cannot fake

  • How many AI startups Nepal has. There is no register.
  • How many state-owned AI-capable computers exist. There is no count.
  • What share of IT exports is AI. Nobody measures it.

Short version: strong on people, thin on products, loud on paper.

Generative AI, agents, automation. What actually changed?

Not the model. We rent that. The job is the harness: a spec, files, typed tools, a budgeted loop, a policy, a trace you can replay.

  1. 1

    spec(requirements)

    What right looks like, in writing. A rubric, a fail case, a stop condition. Before you prompt.

  2. 2

    retrieve(files)

    Search. Rank. Cite the page. No source, no claim. Refuse if it is not in the file.

  3. 3

    bind(tools)

    Add domain-specific tools. Timeouts, retries, idempotency. A tool with no contract is a toy.

  4. 4

    act(once)

    One call, then parse the observation. Did it error, lie, or finish? You decide before the next call.

  5. 5

    loop(budget)

    Plan, act, observe, retry. A step cap and a spend cap.

    planactobs

    retry while budget left · else stop

  6. 6

    guard(policy)

    Allowlist. A payment, a medical call, a fee waits for a human.

  7. 7

    trace(eval)

    Log every call. A golden set. Cost and latency. Replay the miss tomorrow. Without this you have a demo.

How do you land an internship or a first job?

Become a software or data person who can prove they can work end-to-end.

1

Ship

One public project that does a real task. The README says where it fails.

2

Measure/Evaluate

An Evaluation Rubric. Log capabilities and limitations. Evaluate in numbers.

3

Say it

“I built X. It failed at Y. I measured Z.” Practise that out loud this week.

4

Iterate and Optimize

Iterate with new features/bug fixes. Optimize for latency, usability and throughput.

5

Apply

Software/AI and data roles. Talent pools. Referrals. Reach out for mentorship.

  • Vibe coding without code review
  • Building projects with no measurable results or demo/documentation
  • Certificates with no repository
  • Having a GitHub full of unfinished projects
  • Waiting for the "right" project idea

Ethics, privacy, governance, and the road ahead in Nepal.

Four questions for any system. The law is thinner than the duty. That is the road ahead, here and everywhere.

Privacy

What left the room. Who stored the prompt. Who can see it later. Consent is a design choice, not a footer.

Fairness

Who is in the data. Who is not. When it misses, who pays. Errors do not fall evenly.

Accountability

Whose name is on the miss. The vendor, the bank, the ministry, you. If nobody owns it, it is not a system.

Governance

Who wrote the stop. What it may never do. Who can audit the log. A rulebook, or a vibe.

Q&A

Anyone can prompt. Very few people can make an AI system work. That is the career.
